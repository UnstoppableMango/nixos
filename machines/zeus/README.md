# zeus

## Measurements

- 32 threads and 125 GB, with fifteen OSDs at 4 GiB each.

## Hardware

- The SFP+ card is a Broadcom BCM57810 (enp4s0f0 and enp4s0f1); enp4s0f1 is cabled to the aggregator.
- enp8s0, enp12s0 and enp13s0 stay unconfigured.
  Two of the board's NICs carried the previous cluster's 10.69.0.0/16 network.
